<article id="land">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>The land</h2>
      <p class="desc">The overworld is not vanilla's shape, and it is not the only place to go.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Tectonic terrain</h3><p>Mountains run higher and sharper than vanilla raises them, and the ocean floor sits far deeper. A coastline reads as a real one, and a deep ocean is a long way down.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Four seasons</h3><p>The overworld turns through a full year in 96 days. Foliage colour, rainfall and snow all follow it, and so does the temperature you feel.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Caves worth the trip</h3><p>The underground carries biomes of its own on top of vanilla's, and cave exploration has more in it than stone.</p></div>
    <div><h3>The Nether and beyond</h3><p>The Nether has biomes vanilla never had, and The Beyond is a dimension of its own past the two you know.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="structures">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Structures</h2>
      <p class="desc">261 structures across 132 families, spread so that finding one still means
      something.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Variety kept, crowding cut</h3><p>Nearly every structure the pack's mods add is still in the world. What changed is how often they appear: the overworld now places roughly one structure attempt every 77 blocks, against one every 52 before the pass.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Big buildings are rare</h3><p>How far apart a structure sits tracks how large it is. A hut or a ruin is still common, while the 48-block castles, temples and arenas are around twelve times rarer than they were, because those are the ones that made a world feel crowded.</p></div>
    <div><h3>No two in the same place</h3><p>Two structures can no longer generate through each other. A castle growing out of a temple was a normal sight before this and does not happen now.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Level ground only</h3><p>A building generates only where the ground under its whole footprint is flat enough to hold it, measured across the shape it actually builds rather than a circle around its middle. Half-buried keeps and towers hanging off cliffs are gone.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Out of the water</h3><p>Land buildings stay on land. A castle sitting in a river or a village half in the sea came from the mods listing water among the biomes they allow, and both gates that let it happen are closed. Beaches stay open, and the structures that belong underwater are untouched.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Underground stays underground</h3><p>Every buried structure sits deep enough to stay buried under the pack's deeper oceans, so you will not find a dungeon roof breaking through an ocean floor.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
