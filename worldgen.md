  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Terrain</h3><p>Tectonic generates the overworld. Land above sea level stands about an eighth taller than vanilla's, jungles grow stone pillars up to a hundred blocks high, and a river that meets a mountain range carries on underneath it.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Oceans</h3><p>More ocean than vanilla, and deeper. An ordinary ocean floor sits near y 35 and a deep ocean floor near y 5.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Structures</h3><p>249 structures generate, drawn from the pack's mods and from vanilla.</p></div>
    <div><h3>How Often</h3><p>Roughly one structure attempt per 77 blocks. The larger the building the rarer it is, so the 48-block castles, temples and arenas are the rarest things in the world.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Where They Sit</h3><p>A structure needs flat ground across its whole footprint, cannot overlap another, and does not generate in a river or an ocean. Beaches are open, and the structures built for water are exempt. Underground ones start at y -20 or lower, beneath the deepest ocean floor.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Seasons</h3><p>The overworld runs a 96-day year: four seasons of 24 days, each in three parts of 8. Foliage colour, rainfall and snow all follow it. The Nether and the End have no seasons.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Dimensions</h3><p>Three, as in vanilla. Nether Descent rebuilds the Nether's biomes and The Beyond rebuilds the End's.</p></div>
  </div>
