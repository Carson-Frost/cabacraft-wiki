<article id="land">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Terrain and dimensions</h2>
      <p class="desc">What generates the ground and what lies off the overworld.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Tectonic</h3><p>Replaces overworld terrain generation. Mountains reach higher than vanilla's and ocean floors sit far lower, with deep ocean floor near y 5.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Seasons</h3><p>The overworld runs four seasons. A year is 96 days, and foliage colour, rainfall, snow and temperature all follow it.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Cave biomes</h3><p>Galosphere and Spelunker's Charm add underground biomes and cave content on top of vanilla's.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Other dimensions</h3><p>Nether Descent adds Nether biomes. The Beyond is a third dimension beside the Nether and the End.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="structures">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Structures</h2>
      <p class="desc">261 structures in 132 sets, retuned for how often and where they generate.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Density</h3><p>The overworld places one structure attempt per 77 blocks, against 52 before the pass. Vanilla structure sets keep vanilla spacing.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Spacing by size</h3><p>Modded sets are spaced by the size of their largest building. Structures under 100 blocks are 2.3 times rarer than they shipped; those over 1,600 blocks are 12 times rarer.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Overlap</h3><p>Overlap prevention is on. Two structures can no longer generate through each other.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Flat ground</h3><p>A structure generates only where the ground is flat enough across the footprint it actually builds, rather than a fixed radius around its centre.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Rivers and oceans</h3><p>159 land structures are blocked from river and ocean biomes. Beaches stay open, and 35 structures that need water are exempt.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Depth</h3><p>Every underground structure starts at y -20 or lower, so none breaks through the pack's deeper ocean floors.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
