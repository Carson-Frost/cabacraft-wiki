  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Terrain</h3><p>Tectonic replaces overworld generation. Mountains reach higher than vanilla's and ocean floors sit near y 5.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Structures</h3><p>261 structures in 132 sets.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Spacing</h3><p>One structure attempt per 77 blocks, against 52 before the pass. Big buildings are the rarest.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Placement</h3><p>A structure needs flat ground across its own footprint, cannot overlap another, and stays out of rivers and oceans.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Depth</h3><p>Underground structures start at y -20 or lower.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Seasons and dimensions</h3><p>The overworld runs a 96-day year. Nether Descent adds Nether biomes and The Beyond is a third dimension.</p></div>
