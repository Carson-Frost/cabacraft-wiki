---
title: CabaCraft
permalink: /
intro: >-
  A Minecraft modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1 built for a server with three main goals: provide unique
  experiences for different players through Dnd-style [species](/cabacraft-wiki/species/), create
  scarcity that forces collaboration through [classes and specializations](/cabacraft-wiki/classes/),
  and set up a [faction](/cabacraft-wiki/factions/) system that adds another layer and set of
  dynamics on top of the content. There are 263 mods that are intentionally integrated with each
  other and these three main systems that are meant to expand on mechanics and systems in vanilla.
---

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Unique player experiences</h2>
      <p class="desc">Three choices made at the start, which together decide what a player is good at and what they can make.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Species</h3><p>Eleven DnD-style species, each setting its own max health, movement speed and skin and granting powers no other species has. A species changes what your body can do rather than what you can craft, and it is the only one of the three that changes how the world itself treats you.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Classes</h3><p>Fifteen classes, organized under three Paths. A class gates recipes to items no other class can access, and some also grant a bonus or an entire game mechanic on top of that.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Specializations</h3><p>Eight smaller roles, chosen as a secondary craft alongside a class. A specialization works the same way a class does and covers a narrower set of recipes.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Factions and the server</h2>
      <p class="desc">The layer that sits on top of species and class, and the reason scarcity matters.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Three factions</h3><p>Every player belongs to one of three factions, each holding its own territory and its own leader. A faction is where the trading that class gating forces actually happens, because no single player unlocks enough recipes to supply themselves.</p></div>
    <div><h3>The alliance</h3><p>The three factions sit inside one world alliance, whose leader is elected by a vote each faction nominates a candidate for.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Leader-only gear</h3><p>Each faction's signature armor and the alliance set are worn only by the current leader. They cannot be crafted by anyone, they are destroyed on death, and they follow the role rather than the player.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Changes to vanilla systems</h2>
      <p class="desc">The rules that differ most from the base game, in the order they will affect you.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Food</h3><p>An adjusted version of the Valheim food system, and the single largest change in the pack. There is no hunger bar. Each food you eat fills a slot for three minutes and you regenerate health only while a slot is filled, so eating well is how you stay alive rather than how you avoid starving. Food never raises max health, and an empty slot simply stops the healing.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Temperature</h3><p>A temperature system customized per species, so the same biome is survivable for one player and dangerous for another. Biome, weather, time of day and worn armor all move your body temperature and both extremes hurt, which means each faction has to adjust to the environment its territory sits in.</p></div>
    <div><h3>World generation</h3><p>A customized version of Tectonic generates the overworld, with taller land, deeper oceans and rivers that run underneath mountain ranges. Structures from across the mod list are spaced back out so the world does not read as crowded.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Enchanting</h3><p>Thirty-three enchantments are capped, most of them at II, and enchanting runs through Immersive Enchanting rather than the vanilla table. Protection II on a full set cuts 32% of incoming damage where vanilla's Protection IV cuts 64%, so gear matters less and the ladder behind it matters more. Mending is not in the game.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Potions</h3><p>Vanilla brewing is gone and Ars Elixirum replaces it. Only an Alchemist can work a brewing stand or a glass cauldron, so every potion in the world was made by one.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Removed outright</h3><p>Every totem is out of the game, the vanilla Totem of Undying included, and so is every shulker box. A shulker box is portable bulk storage, which undoes the scarcity the class gating is built on.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
