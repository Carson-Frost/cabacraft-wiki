---
title: CabaCraft
permalink: /
intro: >-
  A Minecraft modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1 built for a server with three main goals: provide unique
  experiences for different players through Dnd-style [species](/cabacraft-wiki/species/), create
  scarcity that forces collaboration through [classes and specializations](/cabacraft-wiki/classes/),
  and set up a [faction](/cabacraft-wiki/factions/) system that adds another layer and set of
  dynamics on top of the content. There are 263 mods that are intentionally integrated with each
  other and these three main systems that are meant to expand on mechanics and systems in vanilla.
---

    <div><h3>Species</h3><p>Eleven to choose from. Sets max health, movement speed, skin and powers.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Classes</h3><p>Fifteen, under three Paths. Each gates recipes no other class can reach.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Specializations</h3><p>Eight smaller roles, taken as a second craft alongside a class.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Permanent</h3><p>All three are chosen once, on first entering a world.</p></div>

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Factions and the server</h2>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Three factions</h3><p>Every player belongs to one, each with its own territory and leader.</p></div>
    <div><h3>The alliance</h3><p>All three sit under one alliance leader, elected by a vote.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Leader-only armor</h3><p>Four sets nobody can craft, worn only by the current leader.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Trade</h3><p>No player unlocks enough recipes to supply themselves.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Changes to vanilla systems</h2>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Food</h3><p>An adjusted Valheim food system. No hunger bar; each food fills a slot for three minutes and you regenerate only while a slot is filled.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Temperature</h3><p>Tuned per species, so the same biome is safe for one player and lethal for another. Each faction adjusts to its own territory.</p></div>
    <div><h3>World generation</h3><p>A customized Tectonic. Taller land, deeper oceans, rivers running under mountains, and structures spaced back out.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Enchanting</h3><p>Thirty-three enchantments capped, most at II, through Immersive Enchanting. Mending is out.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Potions</h3><p>Vanilla brewing is gone. Only an Alchemist can brew, so every potion came from one.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Removed</h3><p>Every totem and every shulker box. Portable bulk storage undoes the scarcity.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
