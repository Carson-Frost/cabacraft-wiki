<article id="server">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>The pack</h2>
      <p class="desc">263 mods on NeoForge for Minecraft 1.21.1.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Gated crafting</h3><p>Most recipes belong to one role. A player crafts what their Class and Specialization unlock and trades for the rest.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Race skins</h3><p>Selecting a race replaces the player's skin in world. The Human keeps the default skin, and an Elf takes the skin of the lineage it picks.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Mod list</h3><p>Every mod in the pack is on the Mods page, grouped by what it does.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="character">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Making a character</h2>
      <p class="desc">Five selection screens, in this order, on first entering the world.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Race</h3><p>Eleven races. A race sets max health, movement speed, powers and skin.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Elf Lineage or Human Trait</h3><p>An Elf chooses one of four lineages, a Human one of six traits. The other nine races skip this screen.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Path</h3><p>Combat, Innovation or Creation. The Path determines which Classes the next screen offers.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Class</h3><p>Fifteen Classes across the three Paths. The Class determines which recipes are unlocked.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Specialization</h3><p>Eight Specializations, all offered whatever Path was taken.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="vanilla">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Changes from vanilla</h2>
      <p class="desc">Rules that differ from the base game.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Health and food</h3><p>There is no hunger bar. Eaten food fills timed slots and grants passive regeneration while those slots are full. Max health is ten hearts and food does not raise it.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Potions</h3><p>Vanilla brewing is removed. Ars Elixirum replaces it, and its brewing apparatus is crafted by the Alchemist.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Enchanting</h3><p>Thirty-three enchantments are capped below their vanilla maximum, most of them at II. Mending is not in the game by table, anvil, loot or trade.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Temperature</h3><p>Cold Sweat is active. Biome, weather, time of day, nearby blocks and worn armor all affect body temperature, and both extremes deal damage.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Totems</h3><p>Every totem is removed, the vanilla Totem of Undying included.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Armor</h3><p>All armor across all mods is retuned onto one progression of six rungs, so the range between the weakest and strongest set is narrower than the mods ship.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
