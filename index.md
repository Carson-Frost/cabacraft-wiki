title: CabaCraft

lead: A Minecraft modpack for NeoForge 1.21.1, running 263 mods. You pick a race and a class, and your class decides what you can craft.
---

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Character creation</h2>
      <p class="desc">Five screens, in this order, the first time you enter a world.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li><div><h3>Race</h3><p>Eleven to choose from. Your race sets max health, movement speed, powers and skin.</p></div></li>
    <li><div><h3>Elf Lineage or Human Trait</h3><p>An Elf picks one of four lineages, a Human one of six traits. The other nine races skip this screen.</p></div></li>
    <li><div><h3>Path</h3><p>Combat, Innovation or Creation. Decides which Classes the next screen offers.</p></div></li>
    <li><div><h3>Class</h3><p>Fifteen to choose from, and the largest of these choices. Your Class unlocks most of what you can craft.</p></div></li>
    <li><div><h3>Specialization</h3><p>Eight to choose from, all offered whichever Path you took. A second, smaller craft on top of your Class.</p></div></li>
  </ol>
</article>

<article class="section">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Rules that differ from Minecraft</h2>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Gated crafting</h3><p>Most recipes belong to one role. You craft what your Class and Specialization unlock, and trade for the rest.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Food heals</h3><p>There is no hunger bar. Each food you eat fills a slot for three minutes and you regenerate only while a slot is filled. Food never raises max health, and an empty slot stops the healing rather than hurting you.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Potions</h3><p>Vanilla brewing is gone and Ars Elixirum replaces it. Only an Alchemist can work a brewing stand or a glass cauldron, so every potion in the world was made by one.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Enchanting</h3><p>Thirty-three enchantments are capped, most of them at II. Protection II on a full set cuts 32% of incoming damage where vanilla's Protection IV cuts 64%. Mending is not in the game.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Temperature</h3><p>Your body has a temperature. Biome, weather, time of day and the armor you wear all move it, and you take damage at either extreme.</p></div>
    <div><h3>No totems</h3><p>Every totem is out of the game, the vanilla Totem of Undying included.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
