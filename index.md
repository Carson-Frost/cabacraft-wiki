      <h2>Making a character</h2>
      <p class="desc">Five selection screens, in this order, on first entering the world.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Race</h3><p>Eleven races. Sets max health, movement speed, powers and skin.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Elf Lineage or Human Trait</h3><p>An Elf chooses one of four lineages, a Human one of six traits. The other nine races skip this screen.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Path</h3><p>Combat, Innovation or Creation. Decides which Classes are offered.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Class</h3><p>Fifteen Classes. Decides which recipes are unlocked.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Specialization</h3><p>Eight, all offered whatever Path was taken.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="vanilla">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Changes from vanilla</h2>
      <p class="desc">Rules that differ from the base game.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Gated crafting</h3><p>Most recipes belong to one role. You craft what your Class and Specialization unlock and trade for the rest.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Food heals</h3><p>No hunger bar. Eaten food fills timed slots and heals you while they last. Max health stays at ten hearts.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Potions</h3><p>Vanilla brewing is gone. Ars Elixirum replaces it, and its apparatus is crafted by the Alchemist.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Enchanting</h3><p>Thirty-three enchantments are capped, most at II. Mending is not in the game.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Temperature</h3><p>Cold Sweat is active. Biome, weather, time of day and worn armor all move your body temperature, and both extremes hurt.</p></div>
    <div><h3>No totems</h3><p>Every totem is removed, the vanilla Totem of Undying included.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
