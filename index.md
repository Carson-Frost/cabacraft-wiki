<article id="server">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>What the server is</h2>
      <p class="desc">A Minecraft world run as a tabletop one. Your race sets your body and your
      skin, and the role you take decides what your hands can make.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Nobody crafts everything</h3><p>Recipes belong to roles. A Blacksmith makes the swords, a Cook makes the meals, an Alchemist makes the potions, and what you cannot make yourself you get from the player who can.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Your race is visible</h3><p>Picking a race changes your skin in world, so the people around you can see what you are before you say a word.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Hundreds of mods, one game</h3><p>The pack is tuned so its mods sit together rather than stack on top of each other: shared materials, one accessory screen, and a single armor ladder across all of them. The Mods page lists every one.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="character">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>Making a character</h2>
      <p class="desc">Five screens in a row, the first time you enter the world.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Race</h3><p>Eleven to choose from, each with its own body, skin and powers, all on the Races page.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Elf Lineage or Human Trait</h3><p>An Elf picks a lineage and a Human picks a trait. Every other race skips this screen.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Path</h3><p>Combat, Innovation or Creation. Your Path decides which Classes you are offered next.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Class</h3><p>The role you craft as, drawn from your Path. The Classes page has all fifteen.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Specialization</h3><p>A second, smaller craft on top of your Class, offered to everyone whatever Path they took.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>

<article id="vanilla">
  <div class="head">
    <div>
      <h2>How the pack differs from vanilla</h2>
      <p class="desc">The rules a player raised on the base game trips over first.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Food is how you heal</h3><p>There is no hunger bar. What you have eaten heals you over time and runs out on a timer, so a player who stops eating stops healing. Eating well does not add hearts; you have ten, the same as everyone.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Potions come from an Alchemist</h3><p>Vanilla brewing is gone and Ars Elixirum replaces it, so a potion is an elixir built out of gathered essences. Every one of them comes out of an Alchemist's hands.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Enchantments stop early</h3><p>Every enchantment has a ceiling well below the one vanilla gives it, so an enchantment sharpens gear rather than replacing it. Mending is not in the game at all, by table, anvil, loot or trade.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Heat and cold can kill you</h3><p>Temperature is a real system. The biome, the weather, the time of day and what you are standing next to all reach you, and what you wear changes how fast they get there.</p></div>
    <div><h3>No totems</h3><p>Not the vanilla Totem of Undying and not any of the ones the mods add. A death is a death.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Armor sits on one ladder</h3><p>Every armor set in the pack, from every mod, is retuned onto a single progression. A late set beats an early one, but no set ends a fight by itself, and armor that slows you down still slows you down.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
