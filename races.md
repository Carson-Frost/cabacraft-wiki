    <div><h3>Hold Breath</h3><p>Hold your breath underwater far longer.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Shell Guard</h3><p>-60% damage from attacks that strike you from behind.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Slow</h3><p>-15% movement speed.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Strong Swimmer</h3><p>+15% swim speed.</p></div>

<article id="ribbit">
  <div class="head">
    <img src="{{ '/faces/ribbit.png' | relative_url }}" alt="">
    <div>
      <h2>Ribbit</h2>
      <p class="desc">Frog-folk. Immune to fall damage and poison, strong swimmers, and able to crouch into a coiled leap, but cold-blooded enough that any weather reaches them twice as fast, and limited to a frog's diet of fish, slimeballs, and insects.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="pow">
    <div><h3>Small Build</h3><p>Short and wide.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Cold-Blooded</h3><p>Your body takes the world's temperature twice as fast, in both directions. Quick to freeze, quick to thaw.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Coiled Leap</h3><p>Crouch to wind up, watching the charge bar fill, then stand to spring up and forward. One second of winding reaches about six blocks.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Insectivore</h3><p>Eats only a frog's diet: fish, slimeballs, spider eyes, worms, butterflies, caterpillars, and bait.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Soft Landing</h3><p>Immune to fall damage.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Poison Immunity</h3><p>Immune to poison.</p></div>
    <div><h3>Webbed Feet</h3><p>+25% swim speed.</p></div>
  </div>
</article>
